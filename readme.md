## 📊 Understanding the Indicators (MA, BB, MACD, RSI)

DOGE Vault Premium includes four essential technical indicators. Each one is toggled on/off with a single click. Here is how to use them effectively.

### 1. Moving Average (MA)
- **20 MA (Blue):** Short-term trend. Price above 20 MA = bullish bias. Below = bearish bias.
- **50 MA (Gold):** Medium-term trend. Use as dynamic support/resistance.
- **How to use:** Wait for the 20 MA to cross above the 50 MA (Golden Cross) for a LONG signal. Cross below (Death Cross) for a SHORT signal.

### 2. Bollinger Bands (BB)
- **Parameters:** 20 period, 2 standard deviations.
- **How to use:** When price touches the **upper band**, it's overbought (possible short). When it touches the **lower band**, it's oversold (possible long).
- **The Squeeze:** When the bands narrow, it signals low volatility and an impending breakout. Wait for the bands to expand to confirm the direction.

### 3. MACD
- **Parameters:** 12, 26, 9.
- **How to use:** The blue line (MACD) crossing above the gold dashed line (Signal) is **bullish**. Crossing below is **bearish**.
- **Histogram:** Green bars = bullish momentum. Red bars = bearish momentum. Watch for the bars to get smaller – this is a sign of weakening momentum.

### 4. RSI (Relative Strength Index)
- **Parameters:** 14 periods.
- **How to use:** RSI above 70 = **Overbought** (potential sell signal). RSI below 30 = **Oversold** (potential buy signal).
- **Divergence:** This is the most powerful way to use RSI. If price makes a higher high, but RSI makes a lower high, it's **Bearish Divergence** (SHORT signal). If price makes a lower low, but RSI makes a higher low, it's **Bullish Divergence** (LONG signal).

## ⚡ Advanced Order Types (OCO, Auto-BE, Trailing Stop)

### What is OCO?
**OCO (One‑Cancels‑Other)** allows you to place two orders at the same time. Example: Place a Buy order above market (for an upward breakout) and a Sell order below market (for a downward breakout). Whichever order triggers first automatically cancels the other.

**Use case:** You are waiting for a breakout from a sideways consolidation. You don't know which way it will break. OCO lets you capture whichever direction price chooses.

### What is Auto-BE (Auto-Breakeven)?
When enabled, Auto-BE automatically moves your **Stop Loss to your Entry Price** as soon as the price moves in your favor by more than 50% of your risk. Example: You enter LONG at 0.2000, SL at 0.1990 (risk 10 pips). Price moves up to 0.2005 (5 pips, or 50% of risk). Auto‑BE immediately moves your SL from 0.1990 to 0.2000. You now risk **$0** on the trade.

### What is Trailing Stop?
When enabled, the Trailing Stop dynamically follows the price as it moves in your favor. It never moves backward. Example: You enter LONG at 0.2000, Trailing Distance 0.0010. Price moves to 0.2020. Your SL moves to 0.2010. Price then moves to 0.2030. Your SL moves to 0.2020. Trailing Stop is the best tool for letting your winners run while protecting your profit.

## 🧮 Risk Management – Never Blow Up an Account

The single most important skill in trading is risk management. DOGE Vault Premium includes a built-in **Position Size Calculator** (in `mini-journal-pro.html`) to help you always trade the right size.

**The 1% Rule:** Never risk more than 1% of your account balance on a single trade.
- **Example:** Your account balance is $1000. 1% = $10.
- **Trade Setup:** You want to open a LONG. Your entry is 0.2000, and your Stop Loss is at 0.1990. Your risk is 10 pips (0.0010).
- **Position Size Calculation:** `$10 risk / 0.0010 risk per lot = 10,000 lots`. Or, if your volume is in lots of 0.1, then `$10 risk / (0.0010 * 0.1) = 10,000 lots / 10 = 1000 lots`. Wait, that's too high for a beginner. Let's simplify: `(Account Balance * Risk%) / (Stop Loss in USDT)`.

**In the Position Size Calculator:**
- **Account Balance:** `1000`
- **Risk%:** `1`
- **Stop Loss%:** `2` (assuming 2% move against you)
- **Result:** It will tell you to trade **0.5 lots** (approx). Now you know exactly how many lots to open.

**Golden Rules:** 
1. Always know your risk before you enter a trade.
2. Never add to a losing position.
3. Take a break after 3 consecutive losses.
4. Always use a Stop Loss.

## 📊 Analytics – Measuring Your Performance

The **Analytics tab** is your performance command center. It automatically calculates:

- **Total Trades:** Number of closed trades.
- **Net P&L:** Total profit minus total loss.
- **Win Rate:** Wins / Total Trades. Aim for 50-60% with a positive Expectancy.
- **Profit Factor:** Gross Profit / Gross Loss. A Profit Factor > 1.5 is excellent.
- **Expectancy:** Average P&L per trade. Positive = your strategy is winning.
- **Max Drawdown:** The largest peak‑to‑trough loss in your equity curve. Keep this below 20% of your account.
- **Sharpe Ratio:** Risk-adjusted return. A Sharpe ratio > 1 is good, > 2 is great.
- **Best Pattern:** The pattern (G-61, T-61, etc.) with the highest win rate in your journal.

**How to use Analytics:** After 20 trades, review the Best Pattern. If one pattern has a 70% win rate while the rest are 50%, stop trading the weaker patterns and focus on the winner.

### 3. "Data is not saving (localStorage empty)."
- **Solution:** Do not use Incognito/Private mode. localStorage is disabled in private mode. Check that your browser allows localStorage (usually enabled by default). Clear cache but keep storage.

### 4. "Cannot connect from my phone to my computer."
- **Solution:** Ensure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi. Check your firewall settings and allow port 8000. Use the correct IP address (not `localhost`). Example: `http://192.168.1.10:8000/`.

### 5. "Import Config failed."
- **Solution:** Make sure `doge-config.json` is in the same folder as the HTML files. Open Settings → Import Config → select the file.

### 6. "The chart is not updating."
- **Solution:** Check if JavaScript is enabled. Refresh the page. If using a mobile browser, try Chrome or Safari.

### 7. "The Scanner shows '—' all the time."
- **Solution:** '—' means no pattern detected. Wait for price action to form a pattern. The Scanner updates every 1-2 seconds. It is not broken.

### 8. "I closed a trade but the position box still shows data."
- **Solution:** Click the 'Close' button again or refresh the page. The position should clear after a successful close.

### 9. "Why is my balance not updating after a trade?"
- **Solution:** Balance updates after a trade is closed (not while open). Check the History tab for the closed trade record.

### 12. "I see 'Serving HTTP on 0.0.0.0' but I can't connect."

- **Solution:** On Windows, add an inbound rule for port 8000. On Mac/Linux, check your system firewall settings.
